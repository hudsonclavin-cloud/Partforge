PartForge v6 — quick guide

Describe a part (or attach a photo). PartForge asks a couple of clarifying questions, then designs a parametric OpenSCAD model, compiles it in your browser, and gives you STL/3MF plus a moving assembly preview for mechanisms.

Workflow

Generate → answer questions → tweak parameters → Refine with follow-ups → inspect → export. Templates work with no API key. Everything renders locally; only AI design calls leave the browser.

Flight grade (⚙ Settings → Design grade)

For machined and engineering parts — a rocket club's couplers, bulkheads, retainers, fins, nose cones. The designer follows an aerospace doctrine (materials, processes, ISO fits, rocketry practice), must declare every dimension a shop will measure with its tolerance, and the program measures the finished geometry against that declaration like a CMM: bores, bolt circles, outside diameters, extents, revolved profiles. Declared engineering checks (pressure walls, fin flutter, thread engagement, bolt shear) are recomputed; mass, CG and inertia come out at the material's density. ⋯ → Manufacturing sheet is the drawing substitute. Honest limit: a mesh is not a STEP file — export the .scad and open it in FreeCAD for STEP, and inspect the machined part to the sheet, not to the mesh.
