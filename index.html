Printer

PartForge v5 — quick guide

Describe a part (or attach a photo). PartForge asks a couple of clarifying questions, then designs a parametric OpenSCAD model tuned for FDM printing, compiles it in your browser, and gives you STL/3MF plus a moving assembly preview for mechanisms.

Workflow

Generate → answer questions → tweak parameters → Refine with follow-ups → inspect overhangs → export. Templates work with no API key. Everything renders locally; only AI design calls leave the browser.
